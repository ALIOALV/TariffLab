Calculation policy

Base duty is calculated only for unambiguous numeric ad valorem General rates imported from the official HTS data. Rates such as Free, specific duty, compound duty, quota-dependent rates, references to other headings, or conditional special-program rates are marked for manual review. The tool does not infer preferential treatment from origin. MPF uses a FY2026 rate of 0.3464%, minimum $33.58 and maximum $651.50 for a qualifying formal entry. HMF uses 0.125% for qualifying ocean imports. Exemptions and applicability must be reviewed. The FY2026 MPF settings are NOT valid for every historical or future entry date; outside Oct 1 2025–Sep 30 2026 the MPF is suppressed until a date-appropriate fee schedule is loaded. For October 2026 entries, the correct FY2027 MPF schedule must be sourced separately.

Not a CBP/ACE system. No live API, automatic regulatory update, legal interpretation, Section 301/232/201 determination, IEEPA determination, AD/CVD, quota, PGA, bond, or preferential-program validation. Outputs are estimates for review, not entry-filing instructions. A rate imported from the HTS is not necessarily the final applicable duty.
